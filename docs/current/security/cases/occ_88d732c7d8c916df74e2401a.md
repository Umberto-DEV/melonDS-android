# Running a DSi ROM with an odd DSP audio queue can crash the emulator

[Security index](../README.md) · [Disposition register](../FINDINGS.csv)

**Canonical disposition:** `CORRECTED_SECOND_SCAN`.

- **Identity and status:** `occ_88d732c7d8c916df74e2401a` · scan `ab26b107-97dd-4557-9c4b-2942e5df879d` · severity `low` · layer `core`. Technical disposition from CSV: CORRECTED_SECOND_SCAN. The Security UI state may remain **Open**; it is separate from the technical disposition.
- **Summary:** Teakra exposes a guest MMIO operation that enqueues one 16-bit audio word at a time, but its production sample clock checks only that the transmit queue is nonempty before removing two words for a stereo sample. A malicious DSi ROM or crafted savestate can leave exactly one queued word and make the second 'front()'/'pop()' operate on an empty 'std::queue', invoking native undefined behavior in the Android emulator process.
- **Attack path:** guest Teakra MMIO or savestate -&gt; one-word 'transmit_queue' -&gt; DSi SPU/I2S sample clock -&gt; 'Btdmp::SampleClock()' -&gt; second empty 'front()'/'pop()'
- **Evidence and proof level:** **static** in the sealed finding (`status not specified`): Independent static source trace of Teakra MMIO, savestate and DSi audio timing; Guest MMIO controls transmit enable and single-word sends; The DSi guest can enable BTDMP transmit and invoke 'Send()' once through adjacent Teakra MMIO cells, creating an odd queue length. Disposition evidence: **recorded post-fix class/suite tests, without an individual dynamic reproduction of the trigger** — Focused regression plus final host/JVM/NDK/ART checks recorded in the second-campaign report. This card is supported by source analysis unless it cites a specific RED/GREEN result; aggregate final checks are not counted as an individual finding GREEN.
- **Counterevidence:** The finding requires DSi/Teakra and I2S transmit to be active. The direct source proof establishes native undefined behavior but not a reliable controlled overwrite.
- **Limitations:** No sanitizer or Android runtime reproduction was executed. Exact libc++ failure mode may vary between builds.
- **Recommended correction:** Require at least two queued words before consuming a stereo sample; on a one-word underrun, consume at most the available word and synthesize the missing channel or return silence. Reject savestates whose queue length exceeds the hardware depth and normalize/document odd lengths.
